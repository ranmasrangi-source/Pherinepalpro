## 4. Content Source, Market Research Lead & Verification Policy (`PH00-F009` & Sections 2, 14, 18)

### 4.1 Mandatory Source Governance Rules
1. **Quarantine of Unverified Market Claims (Section 2):** All 2026 monthly visitor-arrival figures, qualitative infrastructure claims, average spend numbers, and competitor rankings from earlier drafts are classified as **`UNVERIFIED_RESEARCH_LEAD`**. They must never be published in customer-facing UI or investor materials as verified facts until corroborated by primary NTB / Government of Nepal datasets.
2. **Mandatory Source Metadata:** Every official destination, permit, visa, weather, or emergency record in the Official Source Registry (`SRC-*`) must store:
   - `sourceId`, `publisher`, `title` (EN/NE), `urlOrContact`, `geographicScope`, `categoryKey`, `publishedDate`, `lastVerifiedUtc`, `nextReviewDate`, and `confidence` (`VERIFIED_OFFICIAL` | `ADVISORY_TIMESTAMPED` | `UNVERIFIED_RESEARCH_LEAD`).
3. **Freshness & Expiry Policy:**
   - **Weather & Route Advisories (`SRC-DHM-*`):** Must display exact bulletin publication UTC timestamp and an explicit notice: *"Advisory is timestamped and not a real-time life-safety guarantee."* Expired bulletins (>24h old) are flagged `STALE_ADVISORY`.
   - **Visa, Permit & Regulatory Schedules (`SRC-NTB-*`, `SRC-DOIM-*`):** Re-verified every 30 days by Content & Compliance staff.
   - **Emergency Directory (`SRC-TP-*`):** Re-verified every 30 days; always accessible without login.
4. **Prohibition on Unsafe Extrapolation (Section 18):** Neither editorial content nor the AI Travel Assistant (`PH12`) may convert stale, conflicting, or uncertain information into a definitive safety instruction.
