# PheriNepal — Owner Decisions Register (`DECISIONS.md`)

**Document ID:** PNP-GOV-DECISIONS-001  
**Blueprint Reference:** Section 32 & Phase 00 (`PH00-F007`)  
**Status Vocabulary Allowed for Unresolved Owner Decisions:** `PENDING` or `GATED` only.  
**Rule:** No AI coding agent or engineer may convert a technical recommendation into an approved Owner decision. Every decision below remains `PENDING` or `GATED` until the designated Approver records a dated written sign-off.

---

## 1. Unresolved-Decision Workflow (`PH00-F007`)

1. **Identification:** Whenever a product, legal, commercial, operational, or architectural requirement depends on an Owner/Founder choice or external contract/counsel evidence, a `DEC-xxx` record is opened in this register.
2. **Mandatory Fields:** Every record must document: (a) Decision ID & Question, (b) Available Options, (c) Technical, Financial, Legal, and Operational Impact, (d) Responsible Owner & Approver, (e) Current Status (`PENDING` or `GATED`), and (f) Safe Work Continuation Boundary.
3. **Quarantine / Gate Enforcement:** Code paths that depend on a `PENDING` or `GATED` decision must default to the safest non-transactional or restricted state (`COMING_SOON`, `INFORMATION_ONLY`, or `RESTRICTED`).
4. **Approval Transition:** Only a written, dated decision from the named Approver (Product Owner / Founder) with attached documentary evidence (ADR, contract, or legal opinion reference) may transition an item out of `PENDING` or `GATED`.
